I co-founded the collective WP Frequencies in Amstedam together with Sandra Lentini and Corentin Racine with the aim to consolidate the ever-growing creative spirit and music passion within our friends’ circle into an entity that would enable us to organise music events, expose new talents and express ourselves through various forms of art. Joined by upcoming DJs with unique event organisation approaches, scene networks and collective contributions: dj.naif (Guillaume Sautierre), Youknow (Charlotte Tusset), NosyOwl (Arno Polegato), Karua (Karla Vincheva), Deb (David Dela Cruz) and Shaunanonymous (Shauna Bilodeau), WP Frequencies is establishing itself internationally through diversely curated parties, free from genre dogma and attracting distinguished and aspiring DJs alike.
