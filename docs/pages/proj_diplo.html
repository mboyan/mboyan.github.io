Diploria’s vision is to create digitally “grown” product designs with biophilic aesthetics - each instance unique like the natural organism it is inspired from, catering to the richness of individual tastes, means of expression and functional necessities.

S4 desk lamp.
Photo by Michael Jasinski.

An algorithmic system for generating unique nature-inspired lamp shades was conceived by Michael Jasinski. Together with him, I co-developed a design framework for exploring new lamp designs, optimising current ones and procedurally adapting details for assembly and electric component integration.

Several lamp variants are currently sold on online marketplaces such as Catawiki and Adorno. The ultimate goal of Diploria is to fully realise the promise of mass customisation offered by today's digital design and fabrication technology, by enabling users to participate in the conception of their product - not through technical involvement but through a playful interface with a biologically-inspired process. Recreating an emotional connection between a human and digital nature.
