Ethereal Spaces

Collaboration with
Guillaume Sautiere a.k.a. dj.naif
2018, Amsterdam

This video project was created as a visualiser for dj.naif's ambient mix Ethereal Spaces. Over the course of several months, I interpreted distinct soundscape scenes from the mix as algorithmically generated visuals, using Processing.

Beginning with a slow awakening from a snowblind emptiness, the appearance of coarse dendrites guides the awareness into a shimmering fractal realm, which suddenly evaporates into the mists of a stormy scenery. The natural surroundings clear awash in crystal streams that refract visions from past and future. A glinting sound from beyond leads the observer into a cosmos of dancing particles, their choreography shape-shifting between murmurations, swirling streams and the formation of a fetus-like figure. As their pulsations fade, a harmonically oscillating figure emerges from the darkness, emanating beams of light into infinity. The figure gradually becomes a distant memory, as a spiraling flight above a brightly illuminated mountainous landscape becomes a final reality. Spiralling further upwards, the observer begins to perceive the crater-like formation of the mountains, distinguishing a walking human figure projected from above. The endlessly looping, glitched gait of the person becomes ever more concrete as a digital artefact imprinted over the iris-shaped formation.


You can also view it on dj.naif's YouTube channel.

Segments of the video were showcased during Future Intel Radio's live streams in May 2026.
